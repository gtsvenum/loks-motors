About Loks Motors

Obsessed With the Golden Era

Loks Motors was founded on a simple belief: the best-looking cars ever made were built between 1950 and 1999 — and they deserve to be driven, not parked. We restore honest classics for buyers who want turn-key nostalgia, and we build ground-up restomods for those who want vintage steel with modern muscle underneath.

Every car that leaves our shop is sorted, documented, and road-tested. No trailer queens. No shortcuts.
